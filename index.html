Our story

A corner table,
a proper cup,
and no rush.

Brew Haven started with a single second-hand roaster and a stubborn idea: that a neighborhood deserves coffee made with care, not a queue that treats you like a number.

We source beans directly from small farms, roast them in the back every week, and dial in each shot by hand. The result is a cup that tastes like someone actually gave a damn — because we did.

  • Direct-trade, single-origin beans
  • Roasted fresh, on-site, every week
  • Oat, almond & whole milk — no upcharge
The craft

From bean to cup.

Three unhurried steps between a raw green bean and the cup in your hand.

01

Sourced

We buy green beans direct from small farms — a dozen single origins, rotated with the seasons.

02

Roasted

Roasted in small batches out back each week, then rested a few days so the flavor settles.

03

Poured

Ground to order and pulled by hand on a lever machine — no autopilot, no shortcuts.
